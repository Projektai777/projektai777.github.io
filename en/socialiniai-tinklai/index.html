Service

Social media posts and page management

For businesses that want an active page but have no time to write, design and post every week. We write the posts and stories, make the pictures and short videos, use the photos and videos you send us, post on your networks and look after the page. To start, send the form on this page.

Who it suits

  • Shops, restaurants, salons and local services with a page that has gone quiet.
  • Freelancers, coaches and clinics who have no time to post every week.
  • Anyone with photos and videos of their work who wants them turned into finished posts.

What you get

  • Posts and stories with text written for your business.
  • Pictures and short videos made by us with AI help and reviewed before use.
  • Your own photos and videos, used in the posts and stories.
  • Posting on your Facebook and Instagram pages (business pages and professional accounts).
  • Page upkeep: profile information and a posting schedule, included in the post price.

How it goes

  • In the form, tell us your pages and how many posts a week you want.
  • Within 2 days we send you a plan for the posts and stories.
  • You approve the plan, and we start posting.
  • You pay once a week.
  • Price: from €5 a post or story, page management included.
Do you promise views, followers or reach?

No. You get well-made posts and stories on a regular schedule, not a promise about how many people a network shows them to.

Can I send my own photos and videos?

Yes. Send them with the form or when you approve the plan, and we use them in your posts and stories.

Which networks do you post on?

Facebook and Instagram: business pages and professional accounts. There we can post for you without your password.

Do you need my password?

No. You add our business as a partner on your page in Meta's own settings (Meta Business Suite), and you can remove us at any time.

What is not included?

A promise of views, followers or sales, ad spend or paid boosts, and answering your customers' messages and comments. Ads are our separate advertising service.

Is page management an extra cost?

No. Page management is included in the post price.

More about your data

Messages go to Projektai777 (Ignas, Kaunas, Lithuania), info@projektai777.eu. Writing to us is voluntary; without your name, e-mail address and message we cannot reply, and the company is optional. We use your name, e-mail, company and message only to answer your enquiry and to take the steps you ask for towards a contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); other business enquiries we answer on our legitimate interest in replying to letters (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). After you press "Let's start" on an offer page, you can enter details for the contract: the company name, registration number, registered address, e-mail for notices, and the representative's position and full name, or, if you are a private individual, your own e-mail; we ask for the other details the contract needs when we write to you – a private individual's full name and address (the address is needed so that the contract makes clear who is entering into it, and so that there is somewhere to send an official letter when we cannot reach you by e-mail). You do not have to provide these details, but without them we will not be able to prepare the contract. We use them only to prepare and carry out the contract and to issue the invoice: a private individual's details so that, at your request, we can prepare to enter into the contract and carry it out (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)), and a company representative's details based on the legitimate interest in entering into and carrying out the contract with the company they represent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); if someone else at the company enters the representative's details, we receive them from that person. We keep and pass on these details in the same way as the correspondence, contracts and invoices described in this notice. If you came from a Google, Facebook or Instagram ad, we see only the ad campaign and the ad itself, and for a search ad also the search keyword. We use this only to count how many messages come from ads (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). The copy on the form's server (Cloudflare) is deleted after 90 days, and we keep the conversation for 12 months after its last e-mail. If we sign a contract, we keep it and the letters that show what was agreed for 10 years after it ends, in case of a legal claim (legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and invoices for 10 years from issue, as Lithuanian accounting rules require (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)). The form and e-mail run on Cloudflare and Google (our mailbox is Gmail). An AI assistant, Anthropic's consumer Claude service, helps us read messages and prepare replies; a simple reply may be sent without a person reading it first. Anthropic may also process this content for its own purposes under its Privacy Policy, such as safety checks and its legal duties. We have switched off its optional model-training setting, but its terms still allow content it flags for a safety review, and feedback we send it, to be used to improve its models. The assistant's session files, with the text of your message and our reply, stay on our computers for about 45 days; its work log (what was written in the chat about your message, such as a summary and reply drafts, with e-mail addresses masked) is kept in our private Google Drive with no set deletion date. TypeSafe (a company in the United States) runs automatic checks for us on our replies and letters, on messages sent to us and on our working notes about an enquiry, for example whether a letter is ready to send or a message needs an answer. On our computers we also keep a log of text sent to TypeSafe and of its answers, with no set deletion date. If you write on Telegram or Messenger, the message is handled by Telegram (a company outside the EU) or Meta under their own rules, and we use it only to reply; please send contracts and personal documents by e-mail. All these companies also process data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Cloudflare and TypeSafe process the data on our behalf, as processors under data processing agreements that form part of their service terms (GDPR Art. 28). Google and Anthropic are providers of the services we use and recipients of the data: we use their consumer services, and their privacy policies name the Irish companies Google Ireland Limited and Anthropic Ireland, Limited as the controllers of EEA users’ data. Under their own terms, Cloudflare, Google and Meta receive data in the US under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (for the transfers covered by their certifications under that framework); in other cases, Cloudflare and Google apply the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. We transfer data to TypeSafe in the US under the same standard clauses, which form part of its data processing agreement. Anthropic transfers data to its servers in the US and arranges that transfer itself: its privacy policy lists adequacy decisions, standard contractual clauses and, in certain cases, derogations provided for by law. Telegram keeps EU users’ data in the Netherlands and may transfer it to its group companies in the British Virgin Islands and Dubai under the same standard clauses. Write to us and we will send you links to these safeguards and a copy of the standard clauses we have concluded ourselves; we will also tell you where to request a copy of the clauses applied by the other providers. The site uses no cookies; your browser only remembers the language you picked. Our server (on Cloudflare) counts page views without cookies, without a counting script in the page and without any code that tells one visitor from another: for each view it notes the day, the page or group of pages, its language, the country, the kind of device (mobile or computer) and the site the visit came from (e.g. Google or Facebook). The country is determined from your IP address and the other details from what your browser sends with every request; neither your IP address nor these browser details are stored: only daily totals are kept, for up to 400 days (legitimate interest in pages and adverts that work, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Cloudflare is a United States company that processes the requests on our behalf; it is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, and the transfer of data to it rests on the European Commission's adequacy decision for that framework. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data or to limit its use, and receive the data you gave us in a commonly used electronic form or have it passed to another controller (info@projektai777.eu). You can object at any time to our use of your data on legitimate interest. You can also complain to a data protection authority.

If we call your company. Independent Projektai777 partners present our services to companies by phone. For this we process the company's name, country, city, field of activity and website address, its publicly listed work phone numbers and e-mail address, the purpose or job title given next to a number (for example, "manager", "sales"), as well as the partner's mark of the call's outcome and the partner's notes on the conversation. We take company data from the open data of state registers, public business directories and the company's own website, and phone numbers only from its website; a partner may add to the list only a company's publicly listed work contacts. We call only companies (legal persons), only on numbers that the company itself publishes as its work contacts, and only in countries whose laws allow calling companies without prior consent; partners must call without hiding their number. These calls are not intended for natural persons — neither private individuals nor those who run a business without having set up a company. A company’s work contact details and a partner’s notes on a call may nevertheless be personal data of its employees or other contact persons, so this notice and the rights it sets out also apply to them. The purpose is to offer our services to the company; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in finding customers (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). We have assessed that your interests and rights do not override this interest: we use only work contacts that the company itself publishes so that it can be contacted; partners are forbidden to record calls; you can opt out with a single sentence during the call. Partners process this data only on our behalf under a signed contract (GDPR Art. 28), call only companies in their own country and have no right to pass the data on to others. The data is kept on our computers, on Cloudflare servers (which run the partners' page) and in Google services (our e-mail and private storage); the AI tools described in this notice (Claude and TypeSafe) help us prepare the list and read the partners' notes. Cloudflare and TypeSafe process this data on our behalf, as processors (GDPR Art. 28), while Google and Anthropic, the provider of the Claude service, receive it as providers of the consumer services we use. All these providers may process data in the United States of America, that is, outside the European Economic Area (EEA); the basis for each provider’s data transfer and how to obtain a copy of the safeguards are set out in the first paragraph of this notice.

How long we keep data. A company stays on the call list as long as it has not opted out, has not written to us itself, has not become our customer and publishes a phone number on its website. We keep the company's name, website and e-mail address and phone numbers while the company is on our list of potential customers, and, of the numbers, only those it itself publishes on its website at the time (we check this about every 30 days). If a company opts out, we delete its phone numbers from the lists used for calling; what stays on the do-not-contact list is set out below. We keep call marks (except the opt-out mark) and partners' notes for 12 months from the entry, and the records under which a partner is owed a reward for a customer they brought in, for as long as claims about the reward can be made (up to 10 years from the end of the contract with the partner; our legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). For the AI tools' logs, what this notice says about them applies. If the company becomes interested, what this notice says about enquiries applies to further contact.

How to opt out. You can object at any time, and without giving a reason, to the processing of your data for direct marketing: tell the partner who calls you or write to info@projektai777.eu. We then no longer contact the company for this purpose by phone or e-mail and no longer show it to partners. On the do-not-contact list we keep only what is needed so that we do not contact the company again and can prove the opt-out: the company's website and e-mail address, its phone numbers, and the date and nature of the opt-out. We process this data to meet our obligation to stop using data for direct marketing (GDPR Art. 21(3), Art. 6(1)(c)) and keep it for as long as we carry out direct marketing to companies. If a company only declines the offer, we also stop calling it. You can also exercise the other rights set out in this notice and lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country.

Right to object. You have the right to object at any time to the uses of your data that rest on our legitimate interest: write to info@projektai777.eu. If we call your company, you have the right to object at any time, and without giving a reason, to the processing of your data for direct marketing: tell the partner who calls you or write to info@projektai777.eu, and we will no longer contact your company for this purpose by phone or e-mail. For the visit statistics you can also switch on the Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal in your browser: requests that carry it are not counted. The statistics hold no visitor identifiers; if we cannot tell which part of them is yours, we will explain why, take into account any information you give us, and act on your objection for whatever we can connect with you.
