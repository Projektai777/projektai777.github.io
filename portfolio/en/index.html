01 — Services

Exactly what we do

Everything in one place. Four directions, and under each one: how it works and what it means for your business.

01

Websites: new and improved

How we improve an existing site: first we review it and list what gets in the way of customers and Google — slow loading, a messy phone layout, invisible in search, hard to contact or order. Then we fix the specific things, rather than rebuilding everything when that is not needed.

  • A new site or an upgrade of the one you have (WordPress or not)
  • Speed, phone screens, visibility in Google (SEO)
  • Booking, inquiry and order forms that actually work
  • Several languages, a content editor so you change texts yourself
  • Price calculators, catalogues, e-shop features
02

Process automation

How it works: we connect the tools you already use — e-mail, Excel, your accounting program, calendar, website — so the repetitive steps happen on their own. A person stays where a decision is needed; the computer does what repeats every day.

  • A mail robot: an incoming invoice or order goes into accounting or a sheet by itself
  • Automatic reminders to customers by SMS or e-mail (visit, payment, deadline)
  • Daily reports waiting in your inbox in the morning
  • Data kept in sync between two systems (e.g. website ↔ accounting)
  • Document generation: contracts, invoices, offers from one template
03

AI solutions

How it works: an AI model is set up to answer from your material or to pull data out of documents. Sensitive data can be processed by a local model on your own computer — nothing leaves it.

  • An assistant that answers customer questions from your price list and rules
  • Invoices, drawings, CVs or inquiries read into a clean table
  • Sorting inquiries and drafting replies
  • Translations and content in several languages
04

Internal systems and tools

When off-the-shelf software does not fit or costs too much, we build a tool made precisely for your work. No monthly fees, no dependence on us: you get all the code.

  • Order and job tracking with statuses and customer notifications
  • Staff panels, estimate and price calculators
  • Loyalty cards on the phone — our own product
  • Customer self-service: order status, documents, bookings

04 — Questions we hear most often

Which digital solutions do you actually mean?

Four things: websites (new or improved), process automation (repetitive work happens by itself), AI tools (customer answers, document reading) and internal systems (order tracking, panels, calculators). Each is described above with examples.

How does process automation work and what can be automated?

We find the work you repeat by hand every day — retyping invoices into accounting, sending reminders, preparing a report — and connect your tools so a program does that step. Anything with clear rules can be automated: mail handling, reminders, moving data, generating documents, reports.

How is a website improved?

We start with a review: speed, the phone layout, visibility in Google, how easy it is to contact and order. You get a list of concrete shortcomings with prices and we fix the ones you choose. Usually nothing has to be rebuilt from scratch.

How much does it cost?

A fixed price, known before we start. Clearly defined modules from 500 EUR, a 50 % advance, the balance after handover. No monthly fees to us.

Will the code be mine?

Yes. After payment you get the full source code and a perpetual right to use it, change it, host it on any server and hand it to another developer.

What if the result does not fit?

We show an intermediate result and change it until it fits. For some solutions we offer a free trial period — you test it in real work and only then pay the balance.
